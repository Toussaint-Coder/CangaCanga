Who we are

CangaCanga (“we”, “us”) is a community ride-sharing app for Burundi. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and your rights.

Data we collect

Account data: full name, phone number, profile photo, optional vehicle plate.

Location data: approximate or precise location when you grant permission, including last known location used to show nearby rides and send nearby-ride alerts.

Ride data: pickup and destination, times, seats, price, notes, vehicle photo.

Device data: push notification token and platform (iOS/Android).

Usage data: reservations, ratings, and in-app notifications related to your trips.

Why we collect it

To create and secure your account, match riders and drivers, show maps and nearby rides, send reservation and safety-related notifications, display profiles to other users when you participate in a ride, improve reliability, and comply with legal obligations.

Sharing

We share limited profile and ride details with other users only as needed to complete a trip (for example driver name, photo, phone, plate, and route labels). We use infrastructure providers (hosting, maps, push delivery, object storage) as processors under contract. We do not sell your personal data.

Retention

We keep your account data while your account is active. After account deletion we remove or anonymize personal data that is no longer needed, except information we must retain for legal, safety, fraud-prevention, or accounting reasons (for example completed trip records that may be required for dispute resolution).

Security

Data in transit is protected with HTTPS/TLS. Access to production systems is restricted. No method of transmission is 100% secure; please use a strong password and protect your device.

Your rights

You may access and update your profile in the app, export a copy of your data, withdraw location/notification/camera permissions in system settings, and delete your account in Profile → Delete account. Contact support if you need help exercising these rights.

Children

CangaCanga is not directed at children under 13. Do not create an account if you are under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at privacy@cangacanga.app. Replace this address with your real support email before store submission if needed.
