Delete your account

You can permanently delete your CangaCanga account and associated personal data. This page is the public account-deletion resource for Google Play and App Store listings.

Delete in the app (recommended)

If you still have CangaCanga installed and can sign in, delete your account from inside the app:

  1. Open the CangaCanga app
  2. Sign in with your phone number and password
  3. Go to the Profile tab
  4. Tap Delete account
  5. Read the notice, confirm, and complete deletion

After deletion you are signed out and must register again to use CangaCanga.

Request deletion by email

If you uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email us to request deletion:

privacy@cangacanga.app

Include:

  • The phone number used for your CangaCanga account
  • Your full name on the account (if you remember it)
  • The subject line: CangaCanga account deletion request

We may ask you to verify ownership of the phone number before completing the request.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, photo, phone number, and login credentials
  • Your open rides (cancelled) and pending reservations
  • Push notification tokens and in-app notifications

What may be retained

Completed trip or review records may be kept in anonymized or legally required form for safety, fraud prevention, or accounting, as described in our Privacy Policy.

How long it takes

In-app deletion is processed immediately when you confirm in the app.

Email requests are typically completed within 7 days after we verify the account.
